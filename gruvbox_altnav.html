Ecosystem Architecture

Pragmatic configurations designed to run efficiently, avoiding modern framework bloating in favor of rock-solid uptime.

The Homelab

My personal proving ground. Powered by reliable hypervisors hosting internal applications and backup targets, managed entirely locally.

  • OS: Debian Server / Alpine
  • Hypervisor: Proxmox VE
  • DNS: Unbound Local Resolver

Networking

Strict segregation of internal assets. Custom firewalls and managed switching separate critical services from untrusted hardware.

  • Gateway: Bare-metal OPNsense
  • Switching: VLAN 802.1Q Tagging
  • Tunneling: WG Site-to-Site
